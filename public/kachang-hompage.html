KA-CHANG

KA-CHANG is a mobile application that connects households with waste collectors. Users can list recyclable or scrap items from their homes, and collectors can view these listings, visit the location, collect the waste, and pay users directly.

Powered by Levitate Studios

How It Works

  • Users list waste items (plastic, paper, metal, etc.) from their home
  • Collectors browse available listings nearby
  • Collectors visit and collect the waste
  • Users receive payment for their scrap items

Who Is It For?

This app is designed for households looking to dispose of waste responsibly and for collectors who want to find and purchase recyclable materials.

Key Features

  • Easy waste listing from your phone
  • Location-based discovery for collectors
  • Direct interaction between users and collectors
  • Promotes recycling and sustainable waste management
